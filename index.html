Why Choose Korean Marine Collagen Peptides?

Superior Bioavailability: Marine collagen peptides are made up of smaller molecules (<1000 Daltons) that are easily absorbed and utilized by the body, up to 1.5x faster than bovine or porcine collagen. This means faster visible results in skin texture, hydration, and elasticity.

Sustainably Sourced & Clean: Our marine collagen is extracted from wild-caught deep-sea fish, free from hormones, mercury, and heavy metal tested.

Rich in Type I & III Collagen: Type I collagen supports skin firmness, hydration, and structure, while Type III collagen helps maintain connective tissues and elasticity. Together, they form a powerful duo for anti-aging and total body support.

How to Incorporate Into Your Daily Routine
